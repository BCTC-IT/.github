# Bay County Tax Collector | Information Technology

Welcome to the official GitHub organization for the **Bay County Tax Collector (BCTC) Information Technology Department**.

The BCTC IT Department is responsible for supporting and maintaining the technology systems and infrastructure that enable the Bay County Tax Collector to provide reliable, efficient, and secure services to the residents of Bay County, Florida.

## About This Organization

This GitHub organization is maintained by the BCTC IT Department for authorized internal use.

All repositories and associated resources are private and accessible only to authorized personnel. Project information, technical documentation, and internal resources are not publicly available.
